# A CONFIRMED broken-impact advisory finding must not be ignored

A finding tagged `CONFIRMED` with `impactIfUnfixed` of `broken` or `unrecoverable`, from any seat, must hold acceptance until an independent mandatory-level reviewer records a finding-specific `block` / `card` / `not-real` ruling. The advisory seat requests that review; its verdict is not itself a mandatory veto. Preserve the existing mandatory/advisory split and all unrelated acceptance gates.

[PR #2835's accepted review](https://github.com/chalbert/web-everything/pull/2835#issuecomment-5869333574) contains a `codex-correctness` finding about merge-resolution work being invisible to `git cherry` in `we:scripts/conveyor/lease-reaper.mjs`. It is tagged `CONFIRMED`, impact `broken`; correctness and security accepted. The PR merged on 2026-09-28 as `629381219c3034e93fc3ff753029a313f9724cdd`. This is evidence of the review failure, not a claim that the lease-reaper defect still exists: current `we:scripts/conveyor/lease-reaper.mjs#defaultGitIsAncestor` includes #4337's merge-commit rejection.

## Design

1. **One typed referral predicate, before advisory filtering.** In `we:scripts/lib/jury-core.mjs`, define the referral from a normalized finding's `verdict === 'CONFIRMED'` and canonical `impactIfUnfixed` equal to `broken` or `unrecoverable`. In `we:scripts/operations/review-pr.mjs`, evaluate all reported seat findings before the `verdictAdmitted` exclusion. A seat's own `carve-out`, `nit`, resolved outcome, captured prevention, or missing prevention must not substitute for the required mandatory ruling. Keep citation/evidence admission intact for ordinary verdict reduction; an off-scope or unverifiable referral goes to the mandatory reviewer for a `not-real` or `card` decision rather than silently disappearing. Do not add `critical` to the enum or infer a level from prose. Missing/invalid tags do not qualify for this new narrow referral; existing fail-closed rules for those values remain in force.
2. **Finding identity and authority are explicit.** Bind each referral and ruling to repository, PR, reviewed head SHA, review run, source seat, and a deterministic finding key derived from its source coordinates and normalized summary. Preserve the originating finding verbatim alongside the normalized record. A ruling contains the key, mandatory reviewer identity and lens, result, rationale, and evidence references; `card` additionally requires an existing durable backlog-card reference. Validate reviewer authority against the actual mandatory roster and existing reviewer-independence checks, not a lens string supplied in finding prose. The reporting advisory seat cannot rule its own referral. A general mandatory `accept`, an author's response, a prevention-filing marker, or a later summary omitting the finding is not a ruling.
3. **Use the PR review record as authority.** Extend the declared operation in `we:scripts/operations/review-pr.mjs` and the bindings in `we:scripts/operations/review-pr-io.mjs` to persist pending referrals and completed rulings as structured, versioned review-comment data with a readable account. Publish the pending record before any effect that could clear acceptance or an awaiting-advisory hold. Keep the run record as resumable execution state; mirror the typed events into the existing jury ledger through `we:scripts/lib/jury-ledger.mjs`, whose fold must expose unresolved referrals and their rulings. Extend event validation/normalization in `we:scripts/lib/jury-core.mjs` so provenance survives serialization. Do not create a rating-store authority or a second independent clearance log. Fresh reads of the durable PR record are required at the label boundary; an unavailable or malformed current-head record with pending referrals fails closed.
4. **Mandatory review is bounded and actionable.** Add one explicit mandatory-review pass over the referral set and the same reviewed diff, using the existing mandatory correctness seat's independent judge machinery. Its mandate requests one `block`, `card`, or `not-real` ruling per referral and explains that `card` requires a recorded card, not an intent to file one. This is a follow-up review of evidence, not a promotion of the advisory seat. Do not recursively refer that pass to itself. If it fails, exhausts its budget, omits a ruling, or cannot establish reviewer authority, persist the remaining keys and park for the operator through the existing `decideParkToHuman` label plan exposed by `we:scripts/review-set-label.mjs`. Mark the operation's confirm as human-addressed as well; `we:scripts/lib/review-loop-policy.mjs` must never auto-answer a pending referral. Show reviewer/owner, reason, finding keys and the resume/ruling action in the durable notice. There is one automated attempt per pending set and reviewed head; retries/resumes reuse the record rather than endlessly dispatching new reviewers.
5. **Rulings discharge only this obligation.** `block` enters the existing mandatory changes/fix/re-review path. `card` releases this referral only after its card is durably readable; `not-real` releases it with the reviewer's evidence-backed rationale. Keep the finding in reports, and do not relabel a deferred defect as fixed. Missing, stale-head, wrong-finding, unauthorized, conflicting or incomplete rulings keep the hold. A later mandatory re-review may supersede a prior ruling explicitly, retaining its history. Head changes invalidate clearance; a same-head duplicate record is idempotent. Other mandatory defects, prevention obligations, degraded-basis gates and human-only policy gates still take precedence.
6. **Enforce at the write boundary as well as the reducer.** In `we:scripts/review-set-label.mjs`, check current-head pending referrals before `accepted`, `restamp`, or `clear-human` can stamp acceptance. A generic clear-human reason alone cannot stand in for the finding-specific ruling. Post and read back the durable ruling before an acceptance label can be installed, preserving the existing comment-before-label ordering and freshness/independence checks. A failed post, unreadable card, changed head or failed read-back leaves the hold intact. Thread the same typed state through `we:scripts/lib/disposition-judge.mjs` so a ledger replay cannot silently lose an unresolved referral. Preserve its existing red-refutation of ordinary outstanding findings; a valid ruling discharges this card's referral without weakening unrelated red grounds.

This does not decide the general advisory-veto question. `we:docs/agent/platform-decisions.md#claim-accuracy-advisory-blocks-on-impact` deliberately limits direct impact blocking; #3338 remains a separate decision. Here the card's existing contract requires a mandatory reviewer's own ruling on a narrowly tagged referral, including an explicit deferral or rejection. Do not expand `MANDATORY_LENSES`, the direct blocking advisory set, or the impact enum as a shortcut.

- Wire the real `we:scripts/operations/review-pr.mjs` path, including optional Codex and Antigravity seats, before their findings are excluded from ordinary reduction. Carry the result through the I/O and label-write seams. Preserve ordinary low-impact and PLAUSIBLE advisory behavior.
- Update the affected source comments to distinguish an advisory verdict from a pending mandatory referral. Reuse existing `accept`, `changes`, `needs-human`, `review:pending` and `review:human` vocabulary; no new verdict or label is required.
- Finish only when restarting the operation and directly attempting an acceptance both observe the same unresolved referral, and every qualifying finding has a durable mandatory ruling before acceptance. Filing a prevention card alone is insufficient.

## Risks

- A core-only fix misses the live all-seat exclusion. Test the declared operation and effect sinks, not only the pure reducer.
- A model's `CONFIRMED` tag is an assertion; it triggers verification, not proof of truth. Preserve evidence admission and require the mandatory reviewer's rationale.
- A transient run flag can disappear on restart or another checkout. The current-head PR record must remain authoritative, with no acceptance on partial persistence.
- Returning `needs-human` without changing confirm ownership can turn the hold into an unattended changes loop. Test the actual human-addressed park and mutually exclusive labels.
- The disposition judge already has stricter red grounds than this referral. Do not weaken them to make a synthetic release test green; release of this obligation does not promise automatic land under every policy.
