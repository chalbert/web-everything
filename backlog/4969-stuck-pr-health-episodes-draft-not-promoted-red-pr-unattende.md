## Design

1. **Policy remains HIGH-only.** Follow we:docs/agent/platform-decisions.md:5711-5719, anchor `automated-health-daemon`: episode dedupe, tracked silences, four-hour high reminder and high-severity OS notification. Keep the existing explicit shadow allowlist mechanism (we:scripts/conveyor/health-watch-core.mjs:433-444). Add draft and red to that list, retain repeated attempts and every other entry. Do not enable agent investigation or filing; their independent gates remain in we:scripts/conveyor/health-watch-core.mjs:463-469.
2. **Proposed threshold: 60 minutes of a continuously observed breaching episode.** This is below both reported unattended intervals (210 and 78 minutes, Progress above), and allows transient stalls to clear. It is a proposal grounded in those two incidents, not a measured optimum. Start at the persisted `firstBreachAt` used by we:scripts/conveyor/health-watch-core.mjs:369-370; all three currently open after one sample (descriptor citations above). It is deliberately separate from draft's 15-minute green floor and red's 60-minute red floor. Consequently a newly detected red episode does not immediately notify merely because CI has been red for an hour; approximately two hours from the failing-check timestamp may elapse before notification. The reported 1.3h unattended interval alone does not prove a retrospective notification; the captured #3373 episode age does exceed 60 minutes at the sampled tick.
3. **Descriptor-driven escalation.** Propose an optional `escalateAfterMs: 60 * MINUTE` on these three descriptors, all with initial `severity: 'medium'`. Draft and repeated attempts therefore intentionally become medium until the duration threshold under this operator direction; other smells retain their severity. Keep breach predicates and hysteresis unchanged. Extra descriptor fields are already accepted by we:scripts/conveyor/health-smells-shape.mjs:21-41. The core must refuse escalation for a non-finite/non-positive threshold or missing/non-finite/future start time; report the episode normally without fabricating age.
4. **One transition, same episode.** Extend `stepEpisodes` at the breaching-sample update (we:scripts/conveyor/health-watch-core.mjs:366-387). On a valid breaching evaluation at age >= threshold, set severity high, persist proposed `escalatedAt`, and emit proposed `escalated` exactly once, keeping key/id/open time. A clean sample resets the medium episode's proposed escalation start to the next breach, even if close hysteresis keeps the episode alive; a closed/reopened episode starts afresh. Unknown/missing evaluations cannot trigger escalation (the existing skip is we:scripts/conveyor/health-watch-core.mjs:357); elapsed wall time may qualify on the next confirmed breach. After escalation, high severity persists until closure. For pre-change state, medium red uses its valid persisted first breach; already-high draft/repeated episodes are grandfathered high with no fresh escalation notification, preserving possible prior delivery. No historical downgrade or replay.
5. **Notify through the existing path.** Handle `escalated` alongside high opening in `planActions`, notify only for high, untracked episodes and permitted shadow membership. Escalation must not re-run open-only diagnosis/investigation/filing. Apply tracked silence before planning, as today (we:scripts/conveyor/health-watch-core.mjs:407-418). For these three smells also enforce high/untracked on reminder and silence-expired notification branches, which currently lack that check (we:scripts/conveyor/health-watch-core.mjs:471-472), so medium cannot leak through that route. Preserve the existing expiry re-raise once high. Coalesce simultaneous escalation/expiry/reminder to at most one notify per key per tick, marking any due reminder consumed; retain the existing four-hour reminder clock (we:scripts/conveyor/health-watch-core.mjs:420-424). The shell already delivers unsuppressed plan entries and honors dry-run/no-notify (we:scripts/conveyor/health-watch.mjs:947-957); no new transport is needed.
